# Science Communication Guides

Starting this year, Modules 1 and 2 and the Tech Report are each concluded with a short interactive **oral report** to your instructor instead of a written lab report. Each oral report is scheduled during the Assessment week following the module (see [schedule](schedule)).

The format is the same for all three: a short lightning talk from a single presentation slide, followed by a discussion with your instructor about your procedure, analysis, uncertainties, and conclusions. Before your meeting, upload your slide to your Physics 50 Google Drive folder, and have your Google Doc lab notebook and Google Data Sheet open and ready — your instructor will likely ask you to walk through specific calculations directly in your spreadsheet.

## Module 1 Oral Report

Your Module 1 oral report is given together with **your lab partner and your instructor**, and is 15 minutes total: a 2-minute lightning talk (from one slide, shared between you and your partner), followed by 13 minutes of discussion. During the discussion your instructor will direct questions to one of you at a time, and either partner should be able to answer in any category — "I did not do that part" is not a complete answer.
